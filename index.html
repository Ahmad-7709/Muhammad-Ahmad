About Me

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science at Stanford University, where I lead the Natural Language Processing and AI Research Group.

My research focuses on developing innovative machine learning algorithms for natural language understanding, with particular emphasis on making AI systems more interpretable and accessible.

Before joining Stanford, I completed my Ph.D. at MIT and worked as a Research Scientist at Google AI. I've published over 50 papers in top-tier conferences including NeurIPS, ICML, and ACL.
